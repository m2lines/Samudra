# Native field plausibility diagnostic

These diagnostics use one OM4-native surface-history initialization (2015-01-03), with OM4 forcings and OM4 truth. They are **not** the pooled, observation-initialized annual score. All nine NPZ files (cooled parent and eight continuation arms) have exactly matching coordinates, channel masks, references and leads; all values are finite. SHA-256 provenance is recorded. Statistics use cosine-latitude weights over each channel's wet mask, an approximate area weighting. Field values are denormalized to physical units by the evaluator.

Fields are SSH, SST, and temperature/salinity at the report's 550 m channel, saved at days 0, 30, 90, 180, and 365. The CSV includes all eight individual draws and their ensemble mean, plus the matching reference. Member numbers at different leads are separate decoder draws and should not be read as coherent stochastic trajectories. Day-zero surface outputs are explicitly anchored to known inputs, so their perfect fit is not evidence of forecast skill.

## Main findings

- **Replay changes the character of the failure, not just its point error.** At day 365, control SST's area-weighted spatial standard deviation is 24.69 C, versus 10.37 C for the reference. The ensemble mean's area-weighted 0.1–99.9 percentile interval is −135.13 to 166.47 C. Replay-bound reduces the standard deviation to 8.87 C and the percentile interval to −6.74 to 34.56 C. Replay alone gives 8.15 C and −5.50 to 34.07 C. The matching reference interval is −1.80 to 30.73 C.
- **The remaining fields are not fully plausible.** Replay-bound's individual SST draws, considering every wet pixel in all eight members, still reach −32.47 and 53.16 C. Its ensemble mean reaches −13.48 and 37.34 C; 1.72% of wet area lies outside the matching reference's full min–max range. Extreme ranges alone can overemphasize isolated pixels; the percentile interval and area fraction are provided alongside them. This is neither a claim that all departures from one reference range are physically impossible nor a calibrated plausibility test.
- **Mean bias is a weak stability detector.** Control SST's global mean is 16.43 C versus reference 18.50 C, even though its spatial standard deviation and local excursions are catastrophic. Positive and negative errors largely cancel. Replay-bound improves the mean to 17.62 C, but its distribution and regional errors remain important.
- **Stability improvements are not only ensemble averaging.** At day 365, average individual-member SST spatial standard deviation is 25.31 C for control, 8.81 C for replay, and 9.40 C for replay-bound. The ensemble-mean values are 24.69, 8.15 and 8.87 C respectively. The catastrophic component is present in the shared mean; it is not removed by averaging eight draws.
- **Improved stability can coexist with distribution contraction.** Day-365 salinity spatial standard deviation is 0.761 in the reference, 2.621 for control, 0.464 for replay and 0.565 for replay-bound. The corresponding average individual-member values are 2.646, 0.517 and 0.604. Day-365 SSH spatial standard deviation is 0.611 m in the reference, 1.549 m for control, 0.472 m for replay and 0.516 m for replay-bound. Lower RMSE has not restored the complete distribution of spatial structure.
- **Spatial losses alone do not stop growth.** Day-365 SST spatial standard deviations are 23.77 C for curvature and 26.88 C for block, compared with 24.69 C for control. Adding replay gives 8.29 C for curvature-replay and 8.87 C for block-replay. These summaries are consistent with the maps and annual errors.
- **The coarse saved leads place the main catastrophe after day 180.** Control's native SST RMSE is 0.74, 1.21, 3.27 and 25.02 C at days 30, 90, 180 and 365. Replay-bound gives 0.83, 1.06, 1.85 and 4.14 C. Replay alone gives 0.81, 1.06, 1.89 and 4.93 C. We cannot determine the precise onset from these sparse snapshots.

## Useful presentation

A four-column lead plot (SST, SSH, T550, S550) with rows for spatial standard deviation relative to the same-lead reference, area-weighted mean bias, and area-weighted 0.1–99.9 percentile envelopes would complement RMSE. Show the ensemble mean and the range across individual members distinctly. Use matching truth at every lead so real seasonal variation is not called instability. Avoid drawing member indices as coherent physical trajectories. A deterministic native forecast under the same inputs would be needed for a direct distributional-growth comparison; none was included here.

The diagnostic covers one origin only and should not be used to rank small differences between replay variants. It establishes a qualitative transition from extreme runaway structure to imperfect, often contracted distributions. It does not establish full physical realism or deterministic-like degradation.
