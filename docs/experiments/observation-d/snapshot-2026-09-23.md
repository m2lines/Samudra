## Purpose and experiment

Adapt the existing deterministic D initializer and five-day ocean evolution model
to observational analyses. This tests whether the pretrained networks can learn
the change of data source without repeating OM4 pretraining. Compare full
fine-tuning against a frozen-network, atmosphere-adapter-only arm and a separately
initialized observation-only arm. Include training-only seasonal climatology,
inferred-state persistence, and inferred-anomaly persistence as controls. Both
persistence controls use each selected initializer as well as the source D
initializer: the former isolate learned dynamics from improvements to the
starting state.

The transferred network architecture and pretrained tensor shapes are retained.
An eight-input, three-output atmospheric adapter supplies learned conditioning
features in the original forcing slots. They are not calibrated physical fluxes.
The observation-only arm has no pretrained weights and uses observation-derived
normalization; its bounded budget does not establish a converged scratch baseline.

## Data and task

OISST SST, DUACS ADT and ERA5 are conservatively coarsened onto D's actual
180 × 360 Gaussian grid, then averaged over explicit five-day intervals. The
one-degree label alone would not identify the same grid. A cell needs finite
source coverage over at least 90% of its full area. Missing targets remain missing;
training-only climatology fills inputs with validity masks.

Monthly IAP temperature and salinity supervise 14 model depth centers through
1850 m. Absolute Salinity is converted to Practical Salinity before coarsening.
OHC references use native IAP depth layers; predictions use native model layers,
with complete-column common support. No observations are invented below 2000 m.
The other model state channels remain for compatibility with the evolution model.

Training uses May 1993–July 2013 (243 target months), validation November
2013–July 2014 (9 months), and held-out reporting January 2015–December 2022
(96 months). The completed materialization and transfer manifests confirm all 348 months,
plus grid and statistics files: 350 NPZ files, 21,888,642,966 bytes (20.385 GiB).
All NPZ SHA-256 hashes were verified on Torch before fitting. Statistics and
climatologies use training samples only.

Each forecast starts from 19 prior five-day surface bins and predicts six or seven
five-day bins with prescribed ERA5. Interior supervision is the overlap-weighted
next-calendar-month mean. Reconstruction and forecasting have different information
availability and must not be presented as the same task.

## Selection and reporting

The selected checkpoint minimizes a frozen validation score:

`0.5 × mean(integrated error / fixed climatology error) + 0.5 × mean(spatial spectral error in dex)`.

Integrated components are SST, geostrophic velocity, EKE, OHC 0–700 m, and OHC
700–2000 m. Surface components use matched 5-, 15-, and 30-day bins. Spectral
components cover SST, ADT and EKE in the North Pacific, Gulf Stream and Agulhas.
The same qualified components and climatology denominators apply to every arm.
Unavailable required components cause failure rather than RMSE-only selection.
Training normalized loss/RMS and physical T/S validation errors are diagnostics.
All checkpoint-selection scores measure forecasts, including checkpoints saved
during reconstruction training. A phase label identifies how the weights were
trained; it does not turn a forecast score into a reconstruction score.

The initial support probe found only three usable spectral bins per region after
excluding wavelengths shorter than four model cells. These measure broad-scale
structure, not mesoscale resolution. The nine-month validation record cannot
support annual variability or long-period temporal spectral selection. Independently
reinitialized forecast segments must not be concatenated as a continuous trajectory.

## Interpretation and discussion after the meeting

This is retrospective, analysis-initialized, ERA5-conditioned prediction. DUACS
smoothing/look-ahead and prescribed atmospheric information are accepted equally
across candidates. Agreement with IAP is agreement with a gridded analysis, not
independent Argo-profile validation. The direct temperature convention remains an
explicit first-pass approximation. One-degree/five-day outputs do not establish
daily or mesoscale forecasting skill.

The next decision should follow the observed comparison: whether full adaptation
beats the frozen-core arm and simple controls on both integrated errors and spectral
structure, whether it benefits from pretraining relative to the bounded scratch arm,
and whether forcing capacity or observation coverage warrants a targeted follow-up.
If a run remains active at capture, report its actual progress and let its approved
budget continue; the deadline is for this snapshot, not for terminating training.
