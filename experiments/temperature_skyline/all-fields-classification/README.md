# All-fields classification at the small model size

This run is submitted; GPU qualification and training results are pending. The run compares a categorical output head with the completed 53.8M-parameter all-fields regression model. The four-block, width-1,024, eight-head backbone and field/depth query embeddings start with exactly the regression model's seeded initial values. Replacing the scalar head with a shared 2,048-logit head brings the total to 55.9M parameters. Inputs remain continuous normalized values, their asinh transforms, wet masks, and geographical/time embeddings. Five queries predict T, salinity, u, and v at one sampled depth, with SSH active only when that depth is the uppermost layer. The target is the absolute field value five days ahead.

Each field/depth channel has 2,048 asinh-spaced centers in its normalized units. Temperature uses the 19 bounds from `torch:/scratch/jr7309/temperature-skyline-20261102/audit-local/support.json`, a complete training-only range audit; the values are copied explicitly into the linked training configuration. Other channels use fixed symmetric bounds of ±16 normalized units, chosen without validation fitting. Assignment boundaries are midpoints between adjacent centers. The first and last categories extend to negative and positive infinity; their point representatives remain the finite endpoint centers. NLL scores categorical probability mass, not a continuous density. Validation reports the fraction of targets assigned to either endpoint category (tail occupancy) and the bin-center quantization MSE so saturation or discretization limits remain visible. Targets receive no density-dependent weighting. The loss retains the regression suite's equal-channel legacy full-grid aggregation and exact corrections for depth/area and coverage sampling: land contributes zero, the spatial denominator includes the full rectangular grid, and all 77 field/depth channels have equal coefficients.

Training starts fresh and stops at 15,000 updates, with effective batch 256 on four GPUs (microbatch 32, accumulation two), totaling 3.84 million accepted crops. It uses the same data-sampling seeds, dates/crops, AdamW settings, and EMA rule as the [regression scaling suite](../all-fields-scaling/README.md). The learning-rate schedule remains the original 25k-update schedule, truncated at 15k; there is no added cooldown. This keeps the LR at each update matched to the regression control. Comparison at 15k uses the regression run's saved validation metrics at 15k, not its 25k endpoint. One seed does not establish a general advantage of classification.

Raw and EMA weights are evaluated at updates 0, 1, 50, 100, 200, 500, 750, 1,000, then every 1,000 through 15,000. Every evaluation uses the same fixed eight validation dates and 64 locations per depth/date as regression. Raw and EMA weight snapshots are retained at every validation point. Curves and CSVs will include per-channel and aggregate categorical NLL, argmax-bin-center MSE/RMSE, probability-mean MSE/RMSE, persistence, quantization MSE, and tail occupancy. The probability-mean prediction is the sum of bin probabilities times their centers in normalized physical units, with no averaging in asinh space. Physical RMSE uses the channel standard deviations; the common MSE comparison uses the legacy full-grid denominator. Raw and EMA best checkpoints are selected separately by aggregate full-grid NLL; the primary final comparison uses fixed update 15k. Early regression measurements exist only at updates 0 and 1 before 1k, so regression curves will not interpolate invented early observations.

A four-update GPU qualification precedes the production run. It checks finite training/validation, the categorical output layout, preserved raw/EMA snapshots, and distributed replica agreement. Training jobs use immutable source archives, preemption recovery, and utilization telemetry. The training configuration is [all_fields_classifier_l04.json](../../../src/samudra/configs/temperature_skyline/all_fields_classifier_l04.json).
